The work

What I offer.

Careful, practical work on original wood trim — from renewing an existing finish to making a matching replacement part.

01 / REFINE

Refinishing

Tired, faded and deteriorated wood trim stripped, refinished and polished. High-gloss lacquer finishing and colour correction where appropriate.

02 / REPAIR

Restoration & repair

Repairing damaged original woodwork, from cracks and chips to failing finishes and damaged veneer, with attention to the original detail.

03 / RECREATE

Veneer & part making

Matching veneer grain and colour, producing replacement components and reconstructing missing or beyond-repair parts.
